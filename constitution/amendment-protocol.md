SkillSpring Constitutional Amendment Protocol

Status: Canonical
Change Control: Self-governing (this document defines the sole modification mechanism)
Scope: Immutable Laws, Authority Boundaries, Global Axioms, Plane Invariants, Enforcement Rules, and all derived constitutional artifacts
Precedence: Absolute for constitutional change

Purpose

This protocol defines the only lawful mechanism by which SkillSpring’s constitutional documents may be modified.

Its purpose is to:

prevent silent drift

prevent informal overrides

preserve auditability and buyer trust

ensure governance changes are intentional, visible, and justified

Any change outside this protocol is invalid by definition.

Amendment Eligibility

Only the following documents are subject to this protocol:

immutable-laws.md

authority-boundaries.md

global-axioms.md

plane-invariants.md

enforcement-rules.md

this amendment protocol itself

Schemas, datasets, and semantics are not constitutional and are governed separately.

Amendment Preconditions

An amendment proposal must include, at minimum:

Change Description
A precise description of what is being modified, added, or removed.

Rationale
Clear justification for why the change is necessary.

Impact Analysis
Explicit analysis of effects on:

authority

enforcement

safety

auditability

downstream implementations

Compatibility Statement
Declaration of whether the amendment is:

backward compatible

forward compatible

breaking

Risk Assessment
Identification of new risks introduced by the change.

Amendment Procedure

All amendments must follow this sequence:

Proposal is drafted and recorded in version control.

Proposal is reviewed against all existing constitutional documents.

Conflicts or violations are resolved explicitly.

Version number is incremented.

Superseded versions are archived.

Amendment is committed as a single, atomic change.

Partial or staged amendments are prohibited.

Approval Authority

Amendment approval authority is explicitly restricted.

Unless formally delegated in writing:

approval authority resides with the SkillSpring constitutional owner

delegation must be explicit, scoped, and revocable

absence of objection does not imply consent

Non-Bypassability

The amendment protocol:

cannot be bypassed

cannot be relaxed temporarily

cannot be overridden by runtime logic, prompts, or tools

Emergency conditions do not suspend this protocol.

Audit and Traceability

Every amendment must:

be traceable to a specific commit

reference the superseded version

preserve historical access to prior versions

remain intelligible to external auditors

History must remain linear and inspectable.

Self-Amendment Constraint

Any modification to this amendment protocol:

must follow this protocol

must explicitly state why the protocol itself is being changed

must include a heightened risk and impact analysis

Circular or implicit self-modification is prohibited.

Effective Date

An amendment becomes effective only when:

committed to version control

versioned explicitly

no conflicting constitutional artifacts remain

Uncommitted or local changes have no authority.

End of Amendment Protocol
